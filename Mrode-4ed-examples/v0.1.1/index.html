Mrode4edExamples.jl

Worked examples in animal breeding and quantitative genetics, written step by step in Julia.

Mrode4edExamples.jl is a companion package to the textbook:

Mrode, R. A., & Pocrnic, T. (2023). Linear Models for the Prediction of the Genetic Merit of Animals (4th ed.). CABI Publishing, Wallingford, UK.

It is written for students learning linear mixed models and genomic prediction, and for instructors teaching from Mrode & Pocrnic (2023). The worked examples of Chapters 4–6, 8–15, and 17–19 are self-contained Julia functions (one function may cover several closely related examples). Each sets up the data in a DataFrame, writes out the Mixed Model Equations (MME) or the key algorithm and intermediate matrices in readable Julia notation, checks them against the general routines in the JuliaBnG packages, and verifies the numerical results against the tables printed in the book:

  • RelationshipMatrices.jl: Pedigree numerator relationship matrix $A$ and its inverse $A^{-1}$ (including unknown parent groups and sire–MGS pedigrees), genetic group contributions $Q$, genomic relationship matrix $G$, single-step inverse $H^{-1}$, APY inverse $G_{\mathrm{APY}}^{-1}$, tuning of $G$ to $A_{22}$, pedigree and genomic dominance $D$, epistatic $G_{AA}$, and breed-specific partial relationship matrices.
  • Breeding.jl: General sparse MME assembly and solvers, prediction error variances, reliabilities and EBV partitions, genomic prediction (SNP-BLUP, GBLUP, BayesA/B/C/C$\pi$), threshold and joint binary–quantitative models, variance component estimation (Henderson's Method 3, EM-REML, AI-REML), Gibbs samplers, covariance functions, and iterative solvers (Jacobi, Gauss–Seidel, iteration on data, and matrix-free PCG).

Highlights

  • 42 Worked Examples Across 14 Chapters: Covers animal, sire, and reduced animal models, unknown parent groups, repeatability and litter models, multivariate models and MACE, maternal and social interaction models, random regression and covariance functions, genomic selection and the Bayesian alphabet, single-step GBLUP and APY, dominance and epistasis, crossbreeding and breed of origin of alleles, threshold models, REML, Gibbs sampling, and iteration on data.
  • Written for Students: Rather than hiding the algebra behind a black-box solver, each script that solves MME first writes them out explicitly as a Julia block matrix ([X'X X'Z; Z'X Z'Z + λ * Ai], [X'Ri*X X'Ri*W; ...], Kronecker forms, or $T'T + \Sigma$) and compares it with mme(...). Pedigrees, phenotypes, and solutions are stored together in DataFrames so you can inspect data and predictions side by side.
  • 392 Automated Numerical Checks: The examples verify both the equivalence of the hand-written equations or algorithms with the library routines and the agreement of the solutions with the numbers printed in the textbook.
  • Documented Textbook Errata: Where a printed table in the 4th edition contains a typographical or arithmetic slip, the example explains the discrepancy and checks the corrected value (see Textbook Discrepancies).

Installation

using Pkg
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/JuliaBnG/Mrode-4ed-examples.jl")

Quick Start

using Mrode4edExamples

# Run Chapter 4, Example 4.1 (Univariate Animal Model)
res = ex_04_1()

# Inspect the pedigree table with records, EBVs, reliabilities, and PA/YD/PC
res.ped

# Inspect the fixed sex effects and breeding value vector
res.b
res.a

To browse the catalogue or run chapters in batch:

# List all 42 examples with their chapter and title
list_examples()

# Run all examples in Chapter 11
run_examples(11)

# Run the entire suite (42 examples, 392 checks) and print a summary
run_examples(; quiet = true)

  • Breeding.jl: Linear mixed models and genomic evaluation.
  • BnGsim.jl: Breeding program simulation pipelines.
  • Mrode4edExamples.jl: Worked textbook examples and numerical benchmarks.

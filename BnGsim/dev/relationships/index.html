Relationships: A, G and realized IBD

This page compares four relationship matrices on one simulated population, and four measures of inbreeding:

  • $A$, the numerator relationship matrix from the pedigree: the expected proportion of the genome shared identical by descent (IBD), twice the coancestry;
  • $I_{\mathrm{seg}}$, the realized IBD relationships: the proportion of the genome at which two animals carry the same founder haplotype, from the founder segments that the loop's crossover records determine (founder_segments, irm). It is exact: no loci are sampled;
  • $I_{\mathrm{locus}}$, the same at the marker loci only;
  • $G$, the genomic relationship matrix of VanRaden (2008), from the genotypes at the markers, centred on the founders' allele frequencies.

$E[I_{\mathrm{seg}} \mid \text{pedigree}] = A$: the pedigree gives the expectation, Mendelian sampling of whole chromosome segments the deviation. $G$ estimates $I_{\mathrm{seg}}$ from identity by state, with a base given by the allele frequencies used for centring.

The population

Model, stated in full: diploid; 3 chromosomes of 40, 30 and 30 Mb with 1 cM/Mb (1 Morgan in all); meiosis with independent assortment and Poisson crossovers (Haldane, no interference); 100 founders with independent random haplotypes at 6000 loci (allele frequency ½, linkage equilibrium in expectation), unrelated and non-inbred by definition; an additive trait (h² = 0.3, QTL every 20th locus); 6 discrete generations of 100 offspring from 10 sires and 25 dams selected by truncation on PBLUP EBV. The scheme records the meioses.

using BnGsim, BnGStructs, RelationshipMatrices, DataFrames, Random, Statistics, LinearAlgebra

sp  = GenericSpecies("tutorial", [40_000_000, 30_000_000, 30_000_000])
rng = Xoshiro(2026)
vm  = VariantMap(sort(unique(rand(rng, 1:100_000_000, 6000))), sp)
hap = Haplotype(BitMatrix(rand(rng, Bool, length(vm), 200)))
mm  = MeiosisMap(sp, vm)
t   = Trait("y"; h² = 0.3)
qtl = eqtl(hap, 1:20:length(vm), t; rng = rng)
pop = founder_population(hap, qtl, t, SimKey(1))
sc  = Scheme(; noff = 100, nsire = 10, ndam = 25, evaluation = :pblup, record_meiosis = true)
summary = simulate!(pop, sc, mm, 6)
summary[:, [:generation, :mean, :F, :Fseg, :Fhet]]

Four relationship matrices

n    = length(pop)
ped  = pedigree(pop)
A    = RelationshipMatrices.nrm(ped.sire, ped.dam)
segs = founder_segments(pop, mm)
Iseg = irm(segs)

# founder labels at the loci, from the segments
labels = zeros(UInt32, length(vm), 2n)
for h = 1:2n, k = segs.ptr[h]:segs.ptr[h+1]-1
    r = searchsortedfirst(vm.pos, segs.start[k]):searchsortedlast(vm.pos, segs.stop[k])
    labels[r, h] .= segs.label[k]
end
Ilocus = irm(labels)

# genotypes of all animals (`gt` is padded to whole words: take the loci);
# G centred on the founders' allele frequencies
nlc = length(vm)
H = genomes(pop)
p = vec(sum(hap.gt[1:nlc, :]; dims = 2)) ./ hap.nhp
G = RelationshipMatrices.grm(H; p = p)

Within a class the pedigree assigns nearly the same $A$ to every pair; the realized relationships spread around it, several times more. Under Haldane's map the spread of half-sib and full-sib relationships has an exact value, which the loop reproduces (validation/ibd.jl): it depends on the genome's length in Morgans, here only 1 Morgan, so the spread is large. $G$ spreads only slightly more than $I_{\mathrm{seg}}$: with 6000 loci, the sampling of loci and identity by state add little.

The realized relationships are also higher than $A$ on average. That is selection, not chance: over 20 replicates of this scheme the mean of $I_{\mathrm{seg}} - A$ over the pairs of the last generation is +0.033 (s.e. 0.008) under truncation on PBLUP EBV and −0.004 (s.e. 0.004) under random selection (validation/ibd-selection.jl). Selected parents carry favourable QTL alleles, so their descendants share the segments around them more often than Mendelian sampling implies; $A$ assumes Mendelian sampling and ignores this. This replicate lies within the spread of the replicates under selection (s.d. 0.034).

$I_{\mathrm{locus}}$ differs from $I_{\mathrm{seg}}$ only by sampling 6000 positions of the genome. $G$ follows $I_{\mathrm{seg}}$ with a regression slope near one because it is centred on the founders' frequencies, the base of the pedigree; centred on the frequencies of the current generation it would be shifted down by the coancestry accumulated since the founders.

Realized inbreeding varies among animals with the same pedigree inbreeding, for the same reason as the relationships, so the pedigree $F$ explains little of it. It is higher than the pedigree $F$ on average, again because of selection (+0.015, s.e. 0.004, over the 20 replicates; +0.001, s.e. 0.003, without selection). Runs of homozygosity of at least 4 Mb (4 cM) estimate the autozygosity in IBD runs of that length, the recent inbreeding; they miss shorter, older autozygous segments. With a marker every 17 kb on average, a run differs from its autozygous segment by a few marker intervals, so here $F_{\mathrm{ROH}}$ and the realized $F$ in runs of at least 4 Mb agree to the precision shown. The diagonal of $G$, centred on the founders' frequencies, follows the realized inbreeding closely with dense markers.

Reproducing

Every random draw is keyed by SimKey(1), so the page gives the same numbers on any machine and number of threads (for a given set of package versions). The variance check of realized relationships against Haldane's map is validation/ibd.jl; segment IBD is validated against A and locus-level IBD in RelationshipMatrices.

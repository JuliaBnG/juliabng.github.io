Results and export

Default result

The default simulation result is a tuple:

haplotypes, chromosome_ends = fisher_wright(100, 100, [1_000_000], 1.0)

Every element of haplotypes is a sorted, unique Vector{UInt32}. A mutation is present when its genome-wide position occurs in the haplotype.

Use muts2bitarray to obtain a dense representation:

using FisherWright

mutations, chromosome_ends = fisher_wright(20, 10, [100_000], 1.0)
alleles, loci = muts2bitarray(mutations, chromosome_ends)
(size(alleles), propertynames(loci))
((0, 40), [:chr, :pos, :ref, :alt, :frq])

alleles is a BitMatrix with loci in rows and haplotypes in columns. loci has these columns:

ColumnMeaning
chrOne-based chromosome index.
posGenome-wide base-pair position.
refRandomly generated reference base.
altRandomly generated alternate base, distinct from ref.
frqAlternate-allele frequency across haplotypes.

Fixed loci are removed by default. Pass flip=true to randomly invert the reference/alternate coding at each locus.

Structured result and substitutions

Set result=true to return a FisherWrightResult. At each fixation_interval, mutations carried by every active haplotype are removed from the active population and recorded as substitutions:

result = FisherWrightResult(
    [UInt32[1, 3], UInt32[2], UInt32[], UInt32[1, 2, 3]],
    UInt32[3],
    UInt32[],
)
(length(result.active_haplotypes), result.chromosome_ends, result.substitutions)
(4, UInt32[0x00000003], UInt32[])

This avoids retaining fixed mutations in every haplotype during long simulations. The result fields are:

FieldMeaning
active_haplotypesPolymorphic mutation positions after fixed loci are removed.
chromosome_endsCumulative chromosome endpoints.
substitutionsSorted fixed mutation positions.
censusCensus sizes $N_0, N_1, …, N_{nt}$ of the run.
malesNumber of males among the parents of each generation.
selected_haplotypesDerived alleles each haplotype carries at the fitness loci; empty in a neutral run.
selected_substitutionsFitness loci whose derived allele was fixed and removed.
mean_fitness, mean_log_fitness, var_log_fitnessFitness of the parents of each generation: mean, and mean and variance of its logarithm.
mean_genotypic, var_genotypic, mean_phenotypic, var_phenotypicUnder TraitFitness, the mean and variance of the genotypic value and of the phenotype of the parents of each generation; NaN otherwise.

Convert a structured result to BnGStructs.Haplotype and a linkage map:

haplotype, loci = to_haplotype(result)
(haplotype.nlc, haplotype.nhp, length(loci.pos))
(3, 4, 3)

Use to_haplotype(result; include_fixed=true) when fixed substitutions should also appear in the dense output. If no polymorphic loci are available, to_haplotype raises an error rather than returning an empty Haplotype.

Census record and effective size

A result returned by fisher_wright records the census size of every generation and the realized number of males among its parents. census[1] is the starting population and census[g + 1] the number of individuals born in generation g; males[g] of the census[g] parents of generation g were males. A run continued from a FisherWrightResult through init appends its generations to the record of that result, so a burn-in and its continuation are numbered as one run; started from plain haplotypes, the record begins anew. A result built by hand, as above, has census holding the number of individuals only and males empty.

sizes = [fill(10, 5); fill(40, 15)]
run = fisher_wright(40, 20, [100_000], 1.0; popsize=sizes, result=true)
(run.census[1:8], run.males[1:8])
([40, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 40, 40], [24, 3, 6, 7, 5, 5, 21, 19])

The record also holds the realized family sizes of every generation: paternal_pairs[g] and maternal_pairs[g] are the numbers of ordered pairs of offspring of generation g that share their sire, $\sum_s m_s(m_s - 1)$, and that share their dam, $\sum_d n_d(n_d - 1)$. Two genes in different offspring therefore derive from the same parent with probability

\[a_g = \frac{\sum_s m_s(m_s - 1) + \sum_d n_d(n_d - 1)}{4N_g(N_g - 1)},\]

$N_g$ the number of offspring, and effective_size returns $1/a_g$ for a generation and the harmonic mean $1/\bar{a}$ for several:

(effective_size(run), effective_size(run, 2:6), effective_size(run, 7:20))
(22.47029168167086, 9.62764863196873, 38.93048128342245)

This holds for every mating scheme and includes the variance of family size that selection adds within a generation. It is the value realized by the pedigree of the run, not an estimate from the genetic data, and it varies from run to run as family sizes do. It depends on the parents: generation 6 above has 40 offspring but the effective size of its 10 parents.

effective_size(run; from=:sexes) uses the numbers of male and female parents only, $a_g = 1/(4N_m) + 1/(4N_f)$, which is the expectation of the realized value under random mating and gives Wright's (1931) $4N_mN_f/(N_m + N_f)$; it does not apply to other mating schemes or to selection. Until version 0.6 this was the only form.

(effective_size(run, 7:20), effective_size(run, 7:20; from=:sexes))
(38.93048128342245, 39.31775396568172)

At constant numbers the asymptotic rate of loss of heterozygosity, $1/(2N_e)$, corresponds to this value plus ½ (see Effective size). Selection that is heritable lowers the effective size further over generations (Robertson 1961), which a record of one generation at a time does not include.

Selected alleles

After a run with selection (see Selection) the derived alleles at the fitness loci are in selected_haplotypes and selected_substitutions, apart from the neutral mutations. to_haplotype(result, loci) returns them as genotypes at the sites of the table, with their true effects:

using Random

chr = [100_000, 100_000]
loci = fitness_loci(chr, 200; s=0.02, μ=5e-4, rng=Xoshiro(1))
sel = fisher_wright(50, 300, chr, 1.0; mut_base=1e6,
                    fitness=SiteFitness(loci), result=true,
                    rng=Xoshiro(2))
qtl, effects = to_haplotype(sel, loci)
first(effects, 5)

Row k of qtl is site loci.pos[k], for every site of the table whether or not it segregates, so the rows line up with the table and with effects. A site whose derived allele was fixed and moved to selected_substitutions is exported as carried by every haplotype (frq = 1); pass include_fixed=false to export it as absent. The dosage of individual i at site k is qtl[k, 2i-1] + qtl[k, 2i].

The result also records, for the parents of every generation, the mean fitness and the mean and variance of log fitness. Entry g describes the same census[g] adults as males[g]. Fitness is absolute, relative to an individual without any derived allele at the fitness loci, so fixed sites count; a neutral run records 1, 0 and 0.

g = [1, 100, 200, 300]
(sel.mean_fitness[g], sel.var_log_fitness[g])
([1.0, 1.2764372172460139, 1.7973075062884818, 2.3524840611591626], [0.0, 0.0012531019756360745, 0.0014114556092230494, 0.002132790851025462])

Under soft selection the mean fitness itself has no effect on the population; it is the quantity that mutation–selection balance and the genetic load are stated in. A lethal genotype among the parents makes the mean of log fitness -Inf and its variance NaN, while the mean fitness stays finite.

To export the causal variants together with the neutral mutations, as sequence data would contain them, add selected=true to the other methods of to_haplotype:

sequence, map = to_haplotype(sel; selected=true)
(size(sequence), count(in(loci.pos), map.pos))
((370, 100), 100)

A neutral mutation can fall on the coordinate of a fitness locus. In the combined export such a coordinate is one locus, derived on a haplotype that carries either; to_haplotype(result, loci) reports the selected allele alone. muts2bitarray(result.selected_haplotypes, result.chromosome_ends) gives the segregating selected sites as a BitMatrix.

Chip extraction

Extract a selected set of marker coordinates directly from a structured result without first materializing every simulated locus:

chip_positions = UInt32[10_000, 50_000, 90_000]
chip_haplotype = to_haplotype(result, chip_positions)
size(chip_haplotype)
(3, 4)

chip_positions must be sorted and unique. Each requested position is retained even when it is absent from the population, in which case its row is all false. extract_chip_bitarray(result.active_haplotypes, chip_positions) returns the corresponding BitMatrix when a BnGStructs.Haplotype wrapper is not needed. A BnGStructs.LocusSet can also select indices from a shared, sorted coordinate vector:

chip_haplotype = to_haplotype(result, locus_set, all_positions)

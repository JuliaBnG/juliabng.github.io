Validation under a changing population size

docs/src/validation/scripts/validate-demography.jl checks popsize against an exact expectation and against msprime DTWF with the same demography. The model is that of the Population genetics validation, $N = 100$ for 2,000 generations from a population without variation; each replicate is then continued through init in two scenarios, and the whole final population is the sample:

ScenarioTrajectory after the burn-inSample
Bottleneck20 individuals for 20 generations, then 100 for 20 generations200 haplotypes
Expansion400 individuals for 100 generations800 haplotypes

The two scenarios share their burn-in, so they are correlated with each other; every check is within one scenario.

Exact expectation of Σ2pq. With $U = \mu L$ new mutations per haplotype per generation (infinite sites) the recursion of Effective size becomes

\[H_B' = (1 - a)H_B + \tfrac{a}{2}H_W + 2U, \qquad H_W' = H_B + 2U,\]

with $a = E[1/(4N_m) + 1/(4N_f)]$ over the sex counts of the parents of that generation, and $\Sigma 2pq = H_W/(2N) + (1 - 1/N)H_B$ for the $N$ individuals of a cohort. It is run from $H = 0$ through the same generations as the simulation, so it assumes neither equilibrium nor an effective size. At the end of the burn-in it gives 395.9, the $\theta(1 - 1/n)$ of the constant-size validation.

msprime with the same demography. Two differences between the models have to be mapped:

  • Effective size. msprime's DTWF picks both parents of an individual from one pool of $N'$ individuals, so a cohort of $N$ two-sex parents is represented by $N' = N_e(N)$: 19.38, 99.48 and 399.50 for $N$ = 20, 100 and 400. $N'$ must be an integer, so each msprime chromosome draws $\lfloor N_e \rfloor$ or $\lceil N_e \rceil$ for every epoch, with probabilities that make the mean $N_e$. The statistics are smooth in $N'$, so their mean over replicates is the value at $N_e$ up to terms of order $(1/N')^2$, below 0.1% here. No statistic is rescaled.
  • Timing. Generation $g$ of FisherWright draws the parents of its $N_g$ offspring from the $N_{g-1}$ individuals of generation $g - 1$. msprime draws the parents of the individuals at time $t - 1$ from the size set for time $t$; this was checked with a population reduced to one individual at time $T$, where all lineages collapse into two genomes at time $T$, not $T + 1$ (msprime 1.4.5). Cohort $g$ is at time $nt - g$, so an epoch that starts with cohort $g_0$ ends, backwards, at time $nt - g_0$, and the earlier size applies from time $nt - g_0 + 1$.

Each scenario has 18 checks at |z| ≤ 3.5: Σ2pq against the recursion, and Σ2pq, $S$, five classes of the whole-population SFS, five classes of the SFS of 20 random haplotypes and five bins of $\sigma^2_d$ against msprime. There is no coalescent $\theta/k$ reference out of equilibrium, so the sample SFS is compared with msprime as well. With 36 checks, the chance that at least one fails by chance is about 2%.

It takes about 2 minutes with 8 threads for 200 replicates:

julia -t 8 validate-demography.jl [reps=100] [seed=2026]

Reading the results of both runs

  • All 36 checks pass for both seeds; the largest |z| is 2.45 (seed
    1. and 2.34 (seed 7), in different statistics.
  • Σ2pq. The bottleneck leaves 277.9 of the 395.9 at equilibrium, and the expansion raises it to 537.2; FisherWright is within 1.7 SE of both in both runs.
  • LD. The bottleneck raises $\sigma^2_d$ in every bin (0.24 against 0.14 at 1–3 Mb at constant size) and the expansion lowers it (0.043); both simulators agree in all bins.
  • Resolution. The standard error of Σ2pq is about 0.7% for the bottleneck and 0.3% for the expansion. The last lines of each run give what that resolves: ten more generations of expansion are 7.7 SE away, but one more generation of bottleneck, or 21 instead of 20 individuals, only 2 to 3 SE. An error of one generation in the bottleneck would therefore not be caught reliably here; the CI test "Heterozygosity through a bottleneck and an expansion" is the check aimed at that, and separates parents from offspring by more than 12 SE.
  • Not covered: growth that changes every generation (growth), fixed sex ratios combined with a changing size (the CI test of heterozygosity decay covers Σ2pq only), and sizes below 20.

The driver is validate-demography.jl; it reuses the statistics of validate-popgen-theory.jl and calls validate-popgen-msprime.py with --epochs, both shown under Driver scripts.

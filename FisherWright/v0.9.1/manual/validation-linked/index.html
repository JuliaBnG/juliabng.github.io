Validation of linked selection

docs/src/validation/scripts/validate-linked.sh compares FisherWright with SLiM 5 (validate-linked.slim) where selection at many linked sites matters: background selection, selective sweeps and the interference between them, which the single-site checks of Validation of selection cannot reach and for which no exact theory exists. The reference is an independent implementation of the same model in SLiM.

Model. $N = 200$ diploid individuals, two sexes with exactly 100 males in every generation (SLiM's WF sexual model; RandomMating(nmale = 100)), soft selection within each sex, 1,600 generations ($8N$) from a monomorphic start. Four chromosomes of 1 Mb and 0.1 Morgan ($M = 10^7$ bp; crossovers Poisson along a chromosome, independent assortment between chromosomes). Neutral mutations at $10^{-6}$ per bp per haplotype per generation. 2,000 selected sites in 20 genes of 10 kb (100 sites each, positions drawn once), five genes per chromosome with centres 200 kb apart; new derived alleles at $2.5 \times 10^{-5}$ per site per haplotype, so $U = 0.1$ per diploid genome. Fitness is multiplicative over sites, $1$, $1 + hs$, $1 + s$.

ScenarioSelected sites
neutral$s = 0$ at every site: the machinery of selected sites without selection

ben and benfree split the sweep scenario into hitchhiking and selection without linkage (see Reading the results); they run only when listed in SCENARIOS. Neutral statistics are always taken over the 4 Mb, with the distance bins of the 2,000 sites.

Matching the site model. FisherWright fixes $s$ per site, and a site is biallelic: a hit on a haplotype that carries the derived allele, or on a fixed site, changes nothing. SLiM draws a new mutation object at every hit. The SLiM model makes the two the same: a mutation() callback gives a new allele the coefficient of its site, reuses the allele object of the site when one is already in the population, and drops a hit on a haplosome that carries it; selected alleles are not converted to substitutions, so a fixed site keeps blocking hits. The report gives how often each case occurs (about 9% of hits fall on a segregating site and 1–2% on a carried or fixed one); without the reuse, those hits would make second alleles of the same site with a fitness of $(1 + hs)^2$ instead of $1 + s$ in a carrier of both.

Neutral sites are biallelic in FisherWright too, and SLiM is matched by its stacking policy and its statistics: a hit on a haplosome that carries an allele at the position is dropped, neutral alleles are not converted to substitutions, and the frequency of a neutral site is the sum over the allele objects at its position. The difference matters at this resolution. When a non-carrier is hit at a segregating site, FisherWright makes the two genes identical, where SLiM without the pooling counts a new, distinct allele, as under infinite sites; the biallelic $\pi_0$ below is 0.2% lower than the infinite-sites one, and under sweeps, which carry many neutral alleles to high frequency, the effect is larger still. An earlier run with SLiM's per-object accounting put FisherWright 0.65% below SLiM in $\pi$ under sweep ($z \approx -3$ in each of two seeds); with the pooling, what is left is 0.23% over four seeds (see Reading the results). The one remaining difference between the models, coefficients kept in single precision in SLiM, is negligible.

Statistics. At generation 1,600, over all $2N$ haplotypes: $\pi$ ($\Sigma 2pq$ per bp, with replacement) and the number of segregating neutral sites $S$, by distance to the nearest selected site on the same chromosome (below 1 kb, which holds the genes, 1–5, 5–20, 20–50 and at least 50 kb); $\pi$ over the genome and the effective size $N\pi/\pi_0$ it implies; neutral substitutions; the selected sites that segregate and that are fixed (deleterious, beneficial), the sum of derived frequencies, and the mean fitness and mean log fitness of the population, fixed alleles included (the load is $1 - \bar w$ under bgs). Here $\pi_0$ is the neutral expectation per bp at generation 1,600 for a biallelic site (ancestral to derived at rate $\mu$, no back mutation), from the drift recursion of Validation of the mating schemes carried over to pairs of genes. For a pair of genes in different individuals (B) or in one individual (W), let $d$ be the probability that exactly one is derived and $b$ that both are, and $q$ the probability that a gene is derived. With $a = 1/N$, drift gives

\[d_B^* = (1 - a)d_B + \tfrac{a}{2}d_W, \qquad b_B^* = (1 - a)b_B + \tfrac{a}{2}(q + b_W), \qquad (d_W^*, b_W^*) = (d_B, b_B),\]

(a coalesced pair is both derived with probability $q$), and then mutation, for either pair,

\[d' = d(1 - \mu) + 2\mu(1 - \mu)(1 - d - b), \qquad b' = b + \mu d + \mu^2(1 - d - b), \qquad q' = q + \mu(1 - q).\]

From a monomorphic start, $\pi_0 = d_W/(2N) + (1 - 1/N)d_B = 7.837 \times 10^{-4}$ at generation 1,600 (the infinite-sites recursion gives $7.853 \times 10^{-4}$, and $4N\mu = 8 \times 10^{-4}$ at equilibrium). The neutral scenario is also compared with it. Each simulator runs 2,000 replicates per scenario; the table gives mean ± SE and the z-score of the difference.

A seed takes about 3.5 hours, with 32 SLiM processes and FisherWright on 16 threads:

docs/src/validation/scripts/validate-linked.sh path/to/slim outdir 2000 2026 32

To run some scenarios only, list them in SCENARIOS:

SCENARIOS="neutral sweep" docs/src/validation/scripts/validate-linked.sh path/to/slim outdir 2000 11 32

// Matched SLiM model for linked selection in FisherWright
// (docs/dev/roadmap.md, validation 7; see validate-linked.jl for the model).
// One population per run: WF, two sexes with exactly N/2 males, parents drawn
// by fitness within each sex, NT generations from a monomorphic start.
// Neutral mutations (m1) at MUN per bp; at each selected site, from the table
// SITES (0-based position, s, h, type 2 deleterious or 3 beneficial), new
// alleles at MUS per haplosome. The coefficient is fixed per site: a new
// allele at a site that already has an allele object in the population
// reuses that object, and a hit on a haplosome that carries it is dropped,
// as in FisherWright's biallelic sites. Neutral sites are biallelic too: a
// hit on a haplosome that carries an allele at the position is dropped
// (stacking policy "f"), and neutral statistics pool the allele objects of a
// position. No allele is converted to a substitution, so fixed ones stay in
// the haplosomes and block new hits, as substituted sites do in FisherWright.
//
// Constants (slim -d): SCENARIO, N, NT, SITES, BINS (0-based start of each
// run of one distance bin, and the bin), MUN, MUS, RBP (crossovers per bp),
// CL (chromosome length), NCHR; the seed with -s. Selected sites at or after
// CL * NCHR (scenario benfree) are each a chromosome of one bp, unlinked from
// the rest; neutral statistics are taken over the first CL * NCHR bp.

initialize() {
    initializeSex();
    // Site table.
    lines = readFile(SITES);
    K = size(lines);
    spos = integer(K);
    ss = float(K);
    st = integer(K);
    for (k in 0:(K - 1)) {
        f = strsplit(lines[k], "\t");
        spos[k] = asInteger(f[0]);
        ss[k] = asFloat(f[1]);
        st[k] = asInteger(f[3]);
    }
    defineConstant("SPOS", spos);
    defineConstant("SS", ss);
    // Distance bins.
    lines = readFile(BINS);
    bs = integer(size(lines));
    bb = integer(size(lines));
    for (k in 0:(size(lines) - 1)) {
        f = strsplit(lines[k], "\t");
        bs[k] = asInteger(f[0]);
        bb[k] = asInteger(f[1]);
    }
    defineConstant("BSTART", bs);
    defineConstant("BBIN", bb);

    initializeMutationType("m1", 0.5, "f", 0.0);
    m1.mutationStackPolicy = "f";
    m1.convertToSubstitution = F;    // a fixed site blocks new hits
    initializeMutationType("m2", 0.25, "f", 0.0);   // deleterious (or s = 0)
    initializeMutationType("m3", 0.5, "f", 0.0);    // beneficial
    for (m in c(m2, m3)) {
        m.mutationStackPolicy = "f";
        m.convertToSubstitution = F;
    }
    initializeGenomicElementType("g1", m1, 1.0);
    initializeGenomicElementType("g2", c(m1, m2), c(MUN, MUS));
    initializeGenomicElementType("g3", c(m1, m3), c(MUN, MUS));
    // Elements: neutral stretches between the selected sites, and each
    // selected site alone; the mutation rate is MUN + MUS at selected sites.
    L = CL * NCHR;
    NX = sum(spos >= L);    // one-bp chromosomes after the main genome
    starts = integer(0);
    ends = integer(0);
    types = integer(0);
    rates = float(0);
    prev = 0;
    for (k in 0:(K - 1)) {
        if (spos[k] > prev) {
            starts = c(starts, prev);
            ends = c(ends, spos[k] - 1);
            types = c(types, 1);
            rates = c(rates, MUN);
        }
        starts = c(starts, spos[k]);
        ends = c(ends, spos[k]);
        types = c(types, st[k]);
        rates = c(rates, MUN + MUS);
        prev = spos[k] + 1;
    }
    if (prev < L) {
        starts = c(starts, prev);
        ends = c(ends, L - 1);
        types = c(types, 1);
        rates = c(rates, MUN);
    }
    for (t in 1:3) {
        w = which(types == t);
        if (size(w))
            initializeGenomicElement(t, starts[w], ends[w]);
    }
    initializeMutationRate(rates, ends);
    // Recombination: RBP per bp within a chromosome; 0.5 between chromosome
    // c and c + 1 (a breakpoint at position x falls between x - 1 and x).
    rends = integer(0);
    rrates = float(0);
    for (c in 0:(NCHR - 1)) {
        if (c > 0) {
            rends = c(rends, c * CL);
            rrates = c(rrates, 0.5);
        }
        rends = c(rends, (c + 1) * CL - 1);
        rrates = c(rrates, RBP);
    }
    for (x in seqLen(NX)) {
        rends = c(rends, L + x);
        rrates = c(rrates, 0.5);
    }
    initializeRecombinationRate(rrates, rends);
    defineGlobal("NEWM", NULL);
    defineGlobal("EVENTS", 0);
    defineGlobal("REUSED", 0);
    defineGlobal("NOOP", 0);
}

// One allele object per selected site, with the coefficient of the site.
function (lo)siteAllele(object<Mutation>$ nm, object<Haplosome>$ hap) {
    defineGlobal("EVENTS", EVENTS + 1);
    ex = sim.mutationsOfType(nm.mutationType);
    ex = ex[ex.position == nm.position];
    if (size(ex) == 0 & size(NEWM) > 0)
        ex = NEWM[NEWM.position == nm.position];
    if (size(ex) > 0) {
        e = ex[0];
        if (hap.containsMutations(e)) {
            defineGlobal("NOOP", NOOP + 1);
            return F;
        }
        defineGlobal("REUSED", REUSED + 1);
        return e;
    }
    nm.setSelectionCoeff(SS[match(nm.position, SPOS)]);
    defineGlobal("NEWM", c(NEWM, nm));
    return T;
}

                    fpos = fpos + 1;
                else

}

# Linked selection in FisherWright against a matched SLiM model
# (validate-linked.slim; docs/dev/roadmap.md, validation 7).
#
# Model: N = 200 diploids, two sexes with exactly N/2 males (SLiM's WF
# sexual model; RandomMating(nmale = N/2)), soft selection within each sex,
# 4 chromosomes of 1 Mb and 0.1 Morgan (M = 10^7 bp), neutral mutation
# 10^-6 per bp per haplotype per generation, NT = 1,600 generations (8N) from
# a monomorphic start. Selected sites: K = 2,000, in 5 "genes" of 10 kb per
# chromosome (100 sites per gene; genes centred 200 kb apart), mutation rate
# 2.5 × 10^-5 per site per haplotype, so U = 2Kμ = 0.1 per diploid genome.
# Scenarios:
#   neutral: s = 0 at every site (the machinery without selection);
#   bgs:     s ~ -Gamma(0.5, 0.04) per site (mean -0.02), h = 0.25;
#   sweep:   as bgs, but 40 random sites beneficial with s = 0.05, h = 0.5;
#   ben:     the 40 beneficial sites of sweep only, in place;
#   benfree: the same 40 sites, each on a chromosome of its own (1 bp, after
#            the 4 Mb), unlinked from the rest: selection without linkage.
# Neutral statistics are taken over the 4 Mb only, with the distance bins of
# the 2,000 sites in every scenario.
# The coefficient is fixed per site, as in FisherWright; SLiM reuses the
# segregating allele object of a site (mutation() callback), so both run the
# same biallelic model; neutral sites are biallelic in both as well (in SLiM,
# allele objects of a position are pooled and none is converted to a
# substitution). Statistics of the whole population at the end:
# Σ2pq per bp of the neutral mutations (π) and the number of segregating
# neutral sites (S) by distance to the nearest selected site, neutral
# substitutions, segregating and fixed selected sites, Σ of derived
# frequencies, mean fitness and mean log fitness (fixed alleles included),
# and Ne from neutral π. For the π rows the theory column is the neutral
# expectation for biallelic sites (neutral_pi below, a = 1/N); its z is
# shown for the neutral scenario.
#
# Usage:
#   julia --project validate-linked.jl setup DIR
#       writes DIR/sites-<scenario>.tsv and DIR/bins.tsv for SLiM;
#   julia --project -t 16 validate-linked.jl compare DIR R SEED [SCENARIOS]
#       runs R FisherWright replicates per scenario and compares them with
#       DIR/<scenario>.tsv from SLiM.

const CHR = fill(CL, NCHR)

const MUN, MUS = 1e-6, 2.5e-5      # neutral per bp, selected per site
const NGENE, GENE, PERGENE = 5, 10_000, 100

# Selected positions (1-based), the same for every scenario.
function positions()
    rng = Xoshiro(20261113)
    pos = Int[]
    for c = 0:NCHR-1, g = 0:NGENE-1
        lo = c * CL + 100_000 + 200_000g - GENE ÷ 2
        append!(pos, lo .+ sort!(randperm(rng, GENE)[1:PERGENE]))
    end
    return sort!(pos)
end

function loci(scen)
    pos = positions()
    K = length(pos)
    if scen in ("ben", "benfree")
        _, ben = loci("sweep")
        p = scen == "ben" ? pos[ben] : NCHR * CL .+ (1:count(ben))
        return FitnessLoci(p, 0.05; h=0.5, μ=MUS), trues(count(ben))
    end
    scen == "neutral" && return FitnessLoci(pos, 0.0; h=0.5, μ=MUS), falses(K)
    rng = Xoshiro(7)
    s = max.(-rand(rng, Gamma(0.5, 0.04), K), -1.0)
    h = fill(0.25, K)
    ben = falses(K)
    if scen == "sweep"
        ben[randperm(rng, K)[1:40]] .= true
        s[ben] .= 0.05
        h[ben] .= 0.5
    end
    return FitnessLoci(pos, s; h=h, μ=MUS), ben
end

# Distance bin of every bp (1-based), to the nearest selected site of the same
# chromosome.
function binof()
    pos = positions()
    b = Vector{Int8}(undef, NCHR * CL)
    for c = 0:NCHR-1
        sp = filter(p -> c * CL < p <= (c + 1) * CL, pos)
        k = 1
        for x = c*CL+1:(c+1)*CL
            while k < length(sp) && abs(sp[k+1] - x) <= abs(sp[k] - x)
                k += 1
            end
            b[x] = searchsortedlast(BINS, abs(sp[k] - x))
        end
    end
    return b
end

function setup(dir)
    mkpath(dir)
    for scen in SCEN
        l, ben = loci(scen)
        open(joinpath(dir, "sites-$scen.tsv"), "w") do io
            for k in eachindex(l.pos)
                println(io, l.pos[k] - 1, '\t', l.s[k], '\t', l.h[k], '\t', ben[k] ? 3 : 2)
            end
        end
    end
    b = binof()
    open(joinpath(dir, "bins.tsv"), "w") do io      # runs of one bin, 0-based starts
        for x in eachindex(b)
            (x == 1 || b[x] != b[x-1]) && println(io, x - 1, '\t', b[x])
        end
    end
end

const NB = length(BINS)
const STATS = [["pi/bp " * l for l in BINLAB]; ["S " * l for l in BINLAB];
    "pi/bp all"; "Ne (pi)"; "neutral subs"; "sel segregating"; "sel fixed -";
    "sel fixed +"; "sum sel freq"; "mean w"; "mean log w"]

# Expected Σ2pq per bp at generation NT under neutrality (a = 1/N, two sexes)
# for biallelic sites: ancestral to derived at rate μ per gene, no back
# mutation, so a hit on a gene that carries the derived allele, or on a fixed
# site, changes nothing. For a pair of genes of different individuals (B) or
# of one individual (W), d is the probability that exactly one is derived and
# b that both are; q is the probability that one gene is derived. The
# infinite-sites recursion (every hit a new difference) is 0.2% higher here.
function neutral_pi()
    a, μ = 1 / N, MUN
    q = dB = bB = dW = bW = 0.0
    mut(d, b) = (d * (1 - μ) + 2μ * (1 - μ) * (1 - d - b), b + μ * d + μ^2 * (1 - d - b))
    for _ = 1:NT
        # B pairs: other individuals (1 - a), one gene of one parent (a/2),
        # the two genes of one parent (a/2); W pairs: a sire and a dam.
        nd, nb = (1 - a) * dB + a / 2 * dW, (1 - a) * bB + a / 2 * q + a / 2 * bW
        (dW, bW) = mut(dB, bB)
        (dB, bB) = mut(nd, nb)
        q += μ * (1 - q)
    end
    return dW / 2N + (1 - 1 / N) * dB
end

# Chromosome lengths: the 4 Mb, and one bp per unlinked selected site.
chrs(scen) = scen == "benfree" ? [CHR; ones(Int, length(first(loci(scen)).pos))] : CHR

function fw_stats(res, l::FitnessLoci, ben, bin, nbp)
    L = NCHR * CL
    haps = res.active_haplotypes
    nh = length(haps)
    cnt = Dict{UInt32,Int}()
    for h in haps, p in h
        p <= L && (cnt[p] = get(cnt, p, 0) + 1)
    end
    H, S = zeros(NB), zeros(NB)
    for (p, c) in cnt
        c < nh || continue
        q = c / nh
        b = bin[p]
        H[b] += 2q * (1 - q)
        S[b] += 1
    end
    π = H ./ nbp
    πall = sum(H) / sum(nbp)
    # Selected sites.
    sel, subs = res.selected_haplotypes, res.selected_substitutions
    idx = Dict(UInt32(p) => k for (k, p) in pairs(l.pos))
    scnt = Dict{UInt32,Int}()
    for h in sel, p in h
        scnt[p] = get(scnt, p, 0) + 1
    end
    seg = count(c -> c < nh, values(scnt))
    fixed = vcat(UInt32.(subs), [p for (p, c) in scnt if c == nh])
    fneg = count(p -> !ben[idx[p]], fixed)
    fpos = length(fixed) - fneg
    sumf = sum(values(scnt); init=0) / nh + length(subs)
    lhom = log1p.(l.s)
    lhet = log1p.(l.h .* l.s)
    base = sum(p -> lhom[idx[p]], subs; init=0.0)
    lw = zeros(nh ÷ 2)
    for i = 1:nh÷2
        a, b = sel[2i-1], sel[2i]
        x = base
        for p in a
            x += insorted(p, b) ? lhom[idx[p]] / 2 : lhet[idx[p]]
        end
        for p in b
            x += insorted(p, a) ? lhom[idx[p]] / 2 : lhet[idx[p]]
        end
        lw[i] = x
    end
    return [π; S; πall; N * πall / neutral_pi(); count(<=(L), res.substitutions); seg; fneg; fpos; sumf;
        mean(exp.(lw)); mean(lw)]
end

function run_fw(scen, R, seed)
    l, ben = loci(scen)
    fit = SiteFitness(l)
    bin = binof()
    nbp = [count(==(b), bin) for b = 1:NB]
    out = zeros(R, length(STATS))
    Threads.@threads for r = 1:R
        rng = Xoshiro(hash((seed, r)))
        res = fisher_wright(N, NT, chrs(scen), 1.0; M=MBP, mut_base=1 / MUN, fitness=fit,
            mating=RandomMating(nmale=N ÷ 2), result=true, rng=rng)
        out[r, :] = fw_stats(res, l, ben, bin, nbp)
    end
    return out, nbp
end

function read_slim(path)
    # SLiM reports every statistic but Ne, which follows from π here.
    v = [parse(Float64, x) for l in eachline(path) if startswith(l, "LINKED\t")
         for x in split(l, '\t')[3:end]]
    v = permutedims(reshape(v, length(STATS) + 2, :))
    k = 2NB + 1                                  # pi/bp all
    stats = hcat(v[:, 1:k], N .* v[:, k] ./ neutral_pi(), v[:, k+1:end-3])
    return stats, v[:, end-2:end]
end

function compare(dir, R, seed, only)
    println("FisherWright ", pkgversion(FisherWright), ", Julia ", VERSION, ", threads ", Threads.nthreads())
    println("N = $N ($(N ÷ 2) males), $NT generations, $NCHR chromosomes of $(CL ÷ 1000) kb and ",
        CL / MBP, " Morgan, μ = $MUN per bp, K = $(length(positions())) selected sites, ",
        "μ_sel = $MUS, R = $R replicates per simulator, seed $seed")
    π0 = neutral_pi()
    @printf("neutral expectation of π per bp at generation %d: %.4e (4Nμ = %.4e)\n\n", NT, π0, 4N * MUN)
    @printf("%-8s %-16s %22s %22s %7s %11s %7s\n", "scenario", "stat", "FisherWright", "SLiM", "z", "theory", "z(FW)")
    for scen in SCEN
        only === nothing || scen in only || continue
        fw, nbp = run_fw(scen, R, seed)
        sl, hits = read_slim(joinpath(dir, scen * ".tsv"))
        println("# ", scen, "  (SLiM replicates: ", size(sl, 1), "; bp per distance bin: ", join(nbp, ", "), ")")
        for (k, name) in enumerate(STATS)
            a, b = fw[:, k], sl[:, k]
            ma, mb = mean(a), mean(b)
            sa, sb = std(a) / sqrt(length(a)), std(b) / sqrt(length(b))
            if sa == 0 && sb == 0
                @printf("%-8s %-16s %22s %22s\n", scen, name, @sprintf("%.5g", ma), @sprintf("%.5g", mb))
                continue
            end
            z = (ma - mb) / sqrt(sa^2 + sb^2)
            ispi = startswith(name, "pi/bp")
            t = ispi ? @sprintf("%.4e", π0) : name == "Ne (pi)" ? @sprintf("%.1f", N) : ""
            zt = (ispi || name == "Ne (pi)") && scen == "neutral" ?
                 @sprintf("%.2f", (ma - (ispi ? π0 : N)) / sa) : ""
            @printf("%-8s %-16s %22s %22s %7.2f %11s %7s\n", scen, name,
                @sprintf("%.5g ± %.3g", ma, sa), @sprintf("%.5g ± %.3g", mb, sb), z, t, zt)
        end
        ev, reused, noop = sum(hits[:, 1]), sum(hits[:, 2]), sum(hits[:, 3])
        @printf("%-8s SLiM selected mutation events: %d; at a segregating site %.2f%%, on a carried or fixed site %.2f%%\n",
            scen, ev, 100reused / ev, 100noop / ev)
    end
end

    setup(ARGS[2])
else
    compare(ARGS[2], parse(Int, ARGS[3]), parse(Int, ARGS[4]), length(ARGS) > 4 ? split(ARGS[5]) : nothing)
end

#!/usr/bin/env bash
# Linked selection in FisherWright against SLiM (validation 7).
#
# Usage: validate-linked.sh SLIM OUTDIR R SEED [JOBS]
#   SLIM    path to the slim executable (SLiM 5)
#   OUTDIR  directory for the site tables, the SLiM output and the report
#   R       replicates per scenario and simulator (at most 9999)
#   SEED    base seed; SLiM replicate r of scenario i uses
#           SEED * 100000 + i * 10000 + r
#   JOBS    SLiM processes run in parallel (default 8)
# The environment variable SCENARIOS restricts the run to some scenarios,
# for example SCENARIOS="bgs"; ben and benfree run only when listed.
# Run from the root of the FisherWright repository.
set -euo pipefail
slim=$1 out=$2 reps=$3 seed=$4 jobs=${5:-8}
here=$(dirname "$0")
mkdir -p "$out"
out=$(cd "$out" && pwd)
scenarios=${SCENARIOS:-"neutral bgs sweep"}
jl="julia --startup-file=no --project"
$jl "$here/validate-linked.jl" setup "$out"
"$slim" -v > "$out/slim-version.txt"
i=0
for s in neutral bgs sweep ben benfree; do
    i=$((i + 1))
    case " $scenarios " in *" $s "*) ;; *) continue ;; esac
    seq 1 "$reps" | xargs -P "$jobs" -I{} sh -c \
        "'$slim' -s \$(( $seed * 100000 + $i * 10000 + {} )) -d \"SCENARIO='$s'\" \
         -d N=200 -d NT=1600 -d \"SITES='$out/sites-$s.tsv'\" -d \"BINS='$out/bins.tsv'\" \
         -d MUN=1e-6 -d MUS=2.5e-5 -d RBP=1e-7 -d CL=1000000 -d NCHR=4 \
         '$here/validate-linked.slim' | grep '^LINKED' > '$out/$s.{}.part'"
    cat "$out/$s".*.part > "$out/$s.tsv"
    rm -f "$out/$s".*.part
done
{
    echo "commit $(git rev-parse --short HEAD), $(head -1 "$out/slim-version.txt"), $(uname -n), $(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%MZ)"
    $jl -t 16 "$here/validate-linked.jl" compare "$out" "$reps" "$seed" "$scenarios"
} | tee "$out/report.txt"
